*Lighting Design, Hardware Design, Software Development (Max/MSP)*

*Dans le bois* was a public art installation by Stéphanie Leduc and Manuel Baumann, built outside of the Mont Royal metro station in Montreal, QC. The work featured two large panels of 350 LED lights, individually controlled via custom software and DMX.

*in collaboration with Jerome Delapierre*
