*Sound Design, Software Development (Max/MSP)*\
\
*SERRA* is an immersive environment for both installation and performance that approaches movement by adapting time and space scales to the activity of plants, and placing the human body within this new dimension. *SERRA* is a collaboration between *Synthesis Center* at *Arizona State University* and *O Vertigo Danse* (Montreal, QC).
