*Visual Design, Software Development (Max/MSP)*

*MindTravel* is an immersive musical experience that takes place in theaters and outdoor spaces. Murray Hidary performs avant-garde real-time piano compositions, with generative visual accompaniments that are responsively influenced by the musical improvisations.
