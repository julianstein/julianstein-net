Julian Stein is a media artist based in Los Angeles, CA. He creates performances and installations that examine relationships between the analog and the digital, primarily through expressions of sound, movement, and light. Through the use of technology, his work aims to reimagine aspects of daily life, highlighting rhythmic and patterned structures to seek out shared qualities between the natural and built world.

MFA from the University of California Los Angeles in Design Media Arts, BFA from Concordia University in Electroacoustic Studies.
